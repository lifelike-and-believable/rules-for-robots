Checks from the process, by the session:

- **One world:** every picture shares the hill, the bushes, the flowers, and the sun. The round tree in 01, 02, and 06 is fuller than the master's, whose middle is dark; the change happens when the treehouse is built over the sketch.
- **Continuity:** 07, 08, and 09 differ by one thing each, so the flag going up and the slide arriving change nothing else.
- **Things connect:** the ladders reach the grass; the slide in 04 covers the top of the ladder, whose foot shows below the slide; the slide in 09 ends on grass.
- **Room for words:** the left half of every picture is open sky and hill.
- **Open:** the box in 02 is small and high; the film may need to push in on it.
