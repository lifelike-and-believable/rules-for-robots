## What changed from the sixth cut, and why

- **Beat 7 gains a second line**, "Rules for Robots teaches your agent to be one." The review found that the film mocks "So you write rules" and is for a product called Rules for Robots, and that it never says what the thing is. This line answers both at the moment the mood turns: the rules are for the robot. The end card's "They teach it what to ask you, and what to just do." goes, because the turn now does that work and the card was crowded.
- **Beat 5 has one note, not two**, and its three questions arrive at full strength. The last question pays off on the end card, so it needs to be read.
- **Beat 8 shows a real sketch.** In the cuts so far the "sketch" was the dashed outline. A pencil drawing of the treehouse on the hill is its own picture, and "Approved." becomes your reply and not a button.
- **Beat 8 ends with a bare flagpole, and beat 9 raises the flag**, as the maintainer asked.
- **Beat 12 is a pull back from the one treehouse to the village**, so the film's treehouse is seen to be part of it, and the four other buildings are the approved designs.
- **The times are what the words need.** The total is 62 seconds. The aim was 45.

## For the maintainer to decide

1. **Length.** Decided on 2026-11-14: "60 seconds sounds good." The master runs about 60 seconds, and a 30-second cut from beats 1, 4, 7, 11, 12, and 13 is made at stage 10.
2. **The turn's second line.** "Rules for Robots teaches your agent to be one." is the session's wording.
3. **The end card's plain line.** "Rules and plugins for Claude Code." names one agent. If the volumes are meant for more than Claude Code, say what it should name.
4. **The order of beats 8 and 9.** The review asked whether "It asks you what only you can decide" should come before "It asks before it builds", so the film answers the hammer questions sooner. The session recommends the order above: the sketch has to be approved before there is a flagpole to choose a flag for.
5. **The note in beat 5.** "KEEP THE TREE." is kept and "Ask before you change anything." is dropped. The dropped one sets up the hammer questions more directly; say if it should be the one that stays.
