## Goals

- Find a specific piece of information, or decide quickly whether this site is relevant.
- Read comfortably on any device.

## Skills and tools

- Arrives from search or a shared link, usually on a phone; may use assistive technology.

## Context of use

Scans the page for a few seconds before deciding to stay. Often has a slow or metered connection.

## Frustrations

- Slow pages, layout shifts, and pop-ups.
- Navigation that hides the thing they came for.
