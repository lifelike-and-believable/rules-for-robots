## Goals

- Publish and update pages without asking a developer.
- Preview a change exactly as visitors will see it.

## Skills and tools

- Comfortable with Markdown or a CMS; not a developer.
- Knows the site's audience and voice.

## Context of use

Updates content weekly and makes urgent fixes on short notice.

## Frustrations

- Build errors with no clear cause.
- Images and links that break silently after an edit.
