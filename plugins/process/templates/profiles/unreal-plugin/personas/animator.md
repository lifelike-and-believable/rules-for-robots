## Goals

- Spend time on performance and timing, not on setup and retargeting chores.
- See the result in the viewport quickly and iterate.

## Skills and tools

- Expert in animation tools (Sequencer, Control Rig, a DCC package); not a programmer.
- Learns tools from short videos and tooltips, rarely from long manuals.

## Context of use

Works shot by shot under deadline, often with many clips open. Needs a tool to work the first time with default settings.

## Frustrations

- Jargon in menus and dialogs that assumes engine internals.
- Losing work to crashes or to operations that silently change assets.
