## Goals

- Reproduce and resolve a customer's problem without engineering help.
- Find the right help page to send a customer.

## Skills and tools

- Knows the product well and the common failure cases; not a developer.
- Works in the app, the help centre, and a ticketing tool side by side.

## Context of use

Handles many tickets a day; needs to see a customer's state quickly and safely.

## Frustrations

- Error messages customers report that do not say what went wrong.
- Help pages that describe an older version of the screen.
