## Goals

- Finish the task they came for quickly, often on a phone.
- Trust that what they entered is saved.

## Skills and tools

- Comfortable with common web apps; not interested in learning this one in depth.
- May use a screen reader, keyboard only, or zoomed text.

## Context of use

Uses the app in short sessions between other work, sometimes on a slow connection.

## Frustrations

- Forms that lose what they typed after an error.
- Unclear errors and dead ends with no next step.
- Being asked for information the app already has.
