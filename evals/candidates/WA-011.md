---
id: WA-011
title: Keep a list of what needs a live test
level: SHOULD
scope: core
verified-by: [review]
targets-failure: unverified-claim
observed-on: []
rationale: Changes verified only against fakes were reported as done and hid a receiver that delivered no data (rules-for-robots#35).
sources: ["practices/working-with-agents.md"]
---
When a change can only be fully verified by a person (live services, hardware, multi-machine sessions), report what you verified and how, and add the remaining check, with exact steps, to the project's needs-a-live-test list named in AGENTS.md. Do not describe such a change as verified or working until that check has been run.
