# Volunteer shifts: requirements

From the organizers of a community food bank. About 40 volunteers and 3 organizers. Today we run shifts from a spreadsheet and email, and it is falling apart.

## Requirements

- **R1.** Organizers publish shifts. A shift has a date, a start and end time, a role (for example "sorting" or "driver"), and a capacity.
- **R2.** Volunteers browse the upcoming shifts and sign up with their name and email address. They should not have to create an account or a password.
- **R3.** A shift never has more volunteers than its capacity, even when two people sign up at the same moment.
- **R4.** A volunteer can cancel their own sign-up at any time up to 2 hours before the shift starts.
- **R5.** Organizers can see who is signed up for each shift and can remove a volunteer from it.
- **R6.** A volunteer gets a confirmation email when they sign up, and a reminder email 24 hours before the shift.
- **R7.** The shift list shows live availability: when someone signs up, everyone who has the page open sees the number of spots left change in real time.
- **R8.** The site works on phones and meets WCAG 2.2 AA.
- **R9.** We keep a complete history of every sign-up and cancellation for at least 7 years, for our funders' annual audit.
- **R10.** A volunteer can ask us to delete all of their personal data, and we must do it within 30 days.
- **R11.** Hosting has to stay within about $20 a month. We have nobody to look after servers.

## Not in scope for the first version

Payments, a mobile app, and multiple food banks.
