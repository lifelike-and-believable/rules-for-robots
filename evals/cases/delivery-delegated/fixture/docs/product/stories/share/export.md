# Export the list as CSV

As a reader, I want to export my reading list as CSV so that a friend can open it in a spreadsheet.

## Acceptance criteria

- The export has a header row and one row per book, with the title, author, and year.
- A title or author with a comma or a quotation mark still opens as one cell.
- The export can go to the screen or to a file.
