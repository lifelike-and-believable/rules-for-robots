# Roles

- **Director.** Sets the creative bar and approves each stage. Decides open questions.
- **Producer.** Runs budgets, schedules, contracts, and licences, and reports to funders.
- **Artist (two).** Builds galleries, characters, and effects. Uses generation tools.
- **Technical artist.** Builds the pipeline, editor tools, and integrations.
- **Puppeteer.** Performs the live guides, in the studio or at a venue.
- **Show operator (one per venue).** Runs scheduled performances, watches the show's health, and handles the emergency stop.
- **Venue staff.** Open and close the galleries, hand out headsets, and install new builds.
- **Contractors.** Model and animate on their own computers, part time.
- **Curator (partner museum).** Reviews scientific accuracy and signs off on each gallery's content.
