# Requirements: performance and characters

- **PRF-1.** A puppeteer in an inertial capture suit drives a guide character's body, face, and hands in real time.
- **PRF-2.** The delay from the puppeteer's movement to the character on the projection is under 80 ms, so the puppeteer can react to visitors naturally.
- **PRF-3.** The puppeteer sees the gallery through a camera feed and hears visitors through a gallery microphone.
- **PRF-4.** AI guides answer visitors' spoken questions in character, using speech recognition, a language model, and synthesized voice.
- **PRF-5.** An AI guide answers within 1.5 seconds of a visitor finishing a question.
- **PRF-6.** A guide can hand over between puppeteer and AI during a session, with a short covering animation.
- **PRF-7.** Character animations, voices, and personality settings are shared by every venue and every output.
- **PRF-8.** For venues on tour, the lead puppeteer can perform remotely from the studio, so the studio does not have to send a puppeteer to every venue.

## Constraints

- Capture data travels between machines in an open format, so recordings can be replayed and edited.
- The capture suit's software runs on Windows only.
