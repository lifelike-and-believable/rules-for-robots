# Requirements: governance, consent, and licensing

- **GOV-1.** Licence terms with lenders and partner museums are approved by the producer, and by no one else.
- **GOV-2.** A performer may withdraw consent at any time, and recordings of their performance are then deleted within 30 days.
- **GOV-3.** The puppeteer's voice is not used to train or clone a synthetic voice.
- **GOV-4.** Each licensed specimen image or scan is used only within its licence's dates and venues.
- **GOV-5.** Provenance records (D4) are permanent and append-only, and cover every asset, including captured performances.
- **GOV-6.** Visitors are told, at each gallery entrance, when a guide is AI-driven.
- **GOV-7.** Unattended sessions record no visitor audio, video, or identifiers.

## Constraints

- The partner museums are in two countries with different privacy law.
