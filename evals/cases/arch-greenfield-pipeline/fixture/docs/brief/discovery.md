# Discovery: decisions and open questions

Decided by the director (approver) after the discovery workshops.

## Decisions

- **D1.** One show build runs everything in a gallery: the projections, the headsets, and the livestream feed.
- **D2.** Live performance and AI guides share the same characters. Switching between them must not be visible to visitors beyond a short transition.
- **D3.** Show mode is separate from production mode. During opening hours the show runs from the venue machines and the operator console only.
- **D4.** Every asset carries a provenance record: who or what made it, from which sources, under which licence.
- **D5.** Integration with venue lighting and audio uses open protocols (OSC, DMX over Art-Net or sACN, timecode), not one adapter per brand.
- **D6.** The studio standard is Unreal Engine 5.8 for all new work.

## Open questions

- **Q1.** Which standalone headset model? Two candidates are in `budget-and-hardware.md`.
- **Q2.** How long may a show restart take before it counts as a stopped show?
- **Q3.** Do the partner museums allow the performer's capture recordings to be kept after the tour?
- **Q4.** Which venues have dependable internet in the galleries? (Venue 1 says "guest Wi-Fi only".)
